# ROAS Formula Breakdown

**Source:** [Instagram Reel @mr.paidsocial](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdMH3nev41_/)
**Added:** 2026-09-12
**Tags:** roas, meta, google, tiktok, performance

## The Formula

```
ROAS = (CTR × CVR × AOV) / (CPM / 1000)
```

Stop looking at ROAS as one number. It's 4 separate levers. Diagnose which is broken, fix that one.

## Each Lever + Fix

| Lever | Problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **CPM too high** | You're overpaying to reach people | Better creative (thumb-stop rate), audience targeting, campaign objective |
| **CTR too low** | People see it but don't click | Hook, headline, creative quality. A/B test first 2 seconds aggressively |
| **CVR too low** | Traffic clicks but doesn't convert | Landing page, offer, price point, trust signals |
| **AOV too low** | Sales happen but don't justify ad spend | Upsells, bundles, pricing tiers |

## AOV Math
- $20 AOV product needs ~7x ROAS to be profitable
- $200 AOV product can survive at 1.5x ROAS
- Easiest lever to move: upsell at checkout before worrying about top-of-funnel
