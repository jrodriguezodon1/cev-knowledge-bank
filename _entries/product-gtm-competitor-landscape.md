# Creator Economy Competitor Landscape

**Source:** Research + outreach (Sep 2026)
**Added:** 2026-09-10
**Tags:** competitors, creatorlookup, orbit, agentio, market

## Campaign Automation
- **Agentio** — $40M Series B (Nov 2025), $340M valuation, 35→100+ people. Forerunner/Benchmark/Craft. Brand-side automation for creator ads. Clients: Uber, DoorDash, Olipop, WHOOP. Enterprise/brand-side buyer. CL is SMB/agency-side — different market.

## Data Providers
- **InsightIQ** — Creator data API. Used historically by BYOB. Potential data enhancement partner for CL.
- **ON Social (onsocial.ai)** — B2B creator data API, 450M profiles, ~$5K/6mo. NOT CreatorIQ reseller. Name-drops GRIN/CreatorIQ as clients. Cold outreach to BYOB Sep 2026.

## Discovery Databases
- **Modash, Influencersclub, Favikon** — $500–$3,000/mo. Keyword + metadata filtering only. None have built embeddings-based discovery as of Sep 2026.

## CEV Positioning
CL at $149/mo is below Modash on price. Play is to leapfrog on capability (agentic ops + vector search) not compete on price. AppSumo cohort will validate whether SMBs pay for autonomous campaign execution vs just better discovery.
