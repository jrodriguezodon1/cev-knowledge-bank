# Business Model Tradeoffs — SaaS vs Usage vs LTD

**Source:** CL product analysis + internal thinking (Sep 2026)
**Added:** 2026-09-10
**Tags:** saas, pricing, business-model, creatorlookup

## Monthly SaaS
- Predictable revenue, investor-friendly
- Wrong model for campaign-trigger workflows (low DAU = churn)
- Right model for daily-use tools

## Usage-Based
- Aligns cost with value delivered
- Right model for agentic ops (pay when campaigns run)
- Harder to predict revenue, but better retention because users pay for outcomes not access

## Lifetime Deal (AppSumo)
- One-time revenue burst, instant cohort
- Users have skin in the game, most vocal/engaged
- Right for beta validation and early feedback loops
- Wrong as primary business model long-term

## The CL Play
- AppSumo LTD → acquisition + validation cohort
- Monthly SaaS stays for standard users
- Usage-based layer added for agentic ops (per agent run / per email sent)
- This is how you get retention without forcing daily use behavior the product doesn't naturally create
