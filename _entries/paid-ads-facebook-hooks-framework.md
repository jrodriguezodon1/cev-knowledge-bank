# High-Performing Facebook Ad Hook Frameworks

**Source:** Instagram Reel — https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dbouu5GO_cb/
**Date logged:** 2026-09-03
**Category:** paid-ads
**Format:** Talking head, iPad with handwritten notes, black caption bar top

---

## The 5 Hook Templates

1. **Avatar callout** — "If you're a [avatar], if you're a [role], if you're a [job title]..."
   - Immediately filters audience, triggers "that's me" response

2. **Problem + symptom** — "The reason you deal with [XYZ problem] is [ABC symptom]."
   - Ex: "The reason you can't close deals is because you don't know how to objection handle."
   - Root cause framing creates curiosity + credibility

3. **Most avatars problem** — "Most [avatars] deal with [problem] because they don't understand [concept]."
   - "Most roofers deal with slim calendars because they don't understand how marketing works."
   - Creates relatability + positions you as the one who DOES understand

4. **Empathy + cause reveal** — "If you've ever struggled with [X], it's because of this."
   - Holds back the answer = forces viewer to keep watching
   - Works for lead gen, VSLs, organic

5. **Direct avatar callout list** — "[Avatar], [role], [profession], [job title] — I don't care who it is, [hook]"
   - Casts wide net within a niche
   - "Roofers, contractors, salespeople — these hooks work damn near every time"

---

## Key Insight
Save this framework. It's not about creativity — it's about specificity of the avatar + empathy with their pain. The more specific the callout, the higher the CTR.

## Relevance to Jose's work
- **CreatorLookup ads:** Use hook #1 and #5 — "Brand managers. Agencies. E-comm teams." callout format
- **ClipHydra:** Hook #4 — "If you've ever launched an ad and wasted budget, it's because of this."
- **Orbit:** Hook #2/#3 — "The reason your ROAS looks great but revenue isn't growing is attribution."
