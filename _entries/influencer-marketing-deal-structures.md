# Deal Structures — Fixed vs Commission vs Hybrid vs CPM

**Source:** Internal BYOB + CreatorLookup operating experience
**Added:** 2026-09-10
**Tags:** deals, influencer, negotiation, pricing

## Deal Types

| Type | Creator Risk | Brand Risk | Acceptance Rate | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fixed Rate | None | High | Highest | Brand awareness, content production |
| Commission/Affiliate | High (no floor) | Low | Lowest | Performance brands with attribution |
| Hybrid (fixed + affiliate) | Low (floor exists) | Medium | High | Best all-around |
| CPM | Medium | Medium | Medium | Large creators where reach = value |

## BYOB Operating Standard
**$400 fixed + 10% affiliate** — gives creators a floor, aligns upside with brand.

## Pricing Benchmarks (2025-2026)
- Nano (1k–10k followers): $50–$300
- Micro (10k–100k): $200–$1,500
- Mid-tier (100k–500k): $1,000–$10,000
- Macro (500k–1M): $5,000–$25,000
- Mega (1M+): $25,000+ — BYOB territory

## Negotiation Playbook
- **Anchor with hybrid.** Fixed gives security, affiliate gives alignment.
- If they push back on fixed → increase affiliate %. 
- If they want pure fixed → price it higher, remove upside.
- **Never do pure commission for quality creators** — they'll deprioritize your brand.
- If they ask about exclusivity → 30-day supplement-category exclusivity is standard. Broader than that, charge more.
