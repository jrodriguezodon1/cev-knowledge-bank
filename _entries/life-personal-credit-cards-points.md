## AA Gold Status
- Re-hold: 40k EQMs/year
- Benefits: complimentary upgrades, priority boarding, free checked bag

## FHR (Fine Hotels & Resorts via Amex Platinum)
Book luxury hotels through Amex FHR for: room upgrade, $100 F&B credit, 4pm checkout, complimentary breakfast. Often cheaper than booking direct once credits factored in.

## MR Transfer Partners Worth Knowing
- Avianca LifeMiles — Star Alliance, often better sweet spots than United
- ANA — exceptional business class awards
- Air France/KLM — Flying Blue, good for Delta flights
- Marriott Bonvoy via Chase UR transfer
